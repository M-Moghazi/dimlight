Help

Set up in under a minute.

Safari keeps new extensions off until you allow them. Here’s where to find the switch.

Turn on Dimlight

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.On iOS 17: Settings › Safari › Extensions.
  2. Tap Dimlight and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. Under Permissions, set All Websites to Allow.
  1. Open the Dimlight app and click Open Safari Settings.
  2. Select the checkbox next to Dimlight.
  3. Click Edit Websites… and set Other Websites to Allow.
A website looks wrong. What can I do?

Open Dimlight from Safari (the page menu on iPhone and iPad, the toolbar button on Mac) and choose Off for that website. Dimlight remembers the choice. Please also report the website so it can be fixed.

A website isn’t dark.

Reload the page. If it still isn’t dark, check that Dimlight is allowed on that website in Safari’s extension settings, or choose Always dark for it.

Does Dimlight work in Private Browsing?

Yes, once you allow it: turn on Allow in Private Browsing in Dimlight’s extension settings.

Why does Dimlight need access to every website?

To change a page’s colors, Safari has to let Dimlight work on that page. Dimlight doesn’t collect, store or send anything about the pages you visit. See the privacy policy.
