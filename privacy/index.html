Privacy Policy

Dimlight · Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Dimlight doesn’t collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no servers. Everything it does happens on your device.

What Dimlight does with the websites you visit

Dimlight is a Safari extension that changes the colors of web pages. To do that, Safari lets it read and adjust the styles of each page you open, after you allow it. Dimlight only changes colors on your screen. It doesn’t record which pages you visit, and it never sends page content, addresses or anything else to the developer or to anyone else.

Some websites keep their styles in separate files. When Dimlight needs one of those files to darken a page, Safari downloads it from the same place the page already loads it from, without your cookies. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: whether Dimlight is on, and the choice you made for individual websites (Auto, Always dark or Off).
  • Per-website results: how Dimlight handled a website, saved so the page darkens instantly the next time.
  • On iPhone and iPad: the time Dimlight last ran in Safari, so the app can show whether it’s on.

This information is kept by Safari and the Dimlight app on your device. It isn’t shared with the developer or anyone else, and deleting Dimlight deletes it.

Third parties

Dimlight contains no third-party code, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn’t share data with third parties.

Children

Dimlight doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
